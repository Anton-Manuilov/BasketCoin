## Details

### AA-1 (High) — feedless stables priced at $1 indefinitely — **Mitigated**
New `freezeStableMint(stable)`: the owner **or guardian** can disable minting with a specific stablecoin instantly,
without the 2-day timelock. Lifting the freeze (`unfreezeStableMint`) goes through the 2-day timelock. This closes the
entry leg of the attack (deposit the discounted stable at $1). Redemption in that stable stays open so holders can always
exit. `previewMint` reports the same `StableMintIsFrozen` error. Residual risk: protection depends on detecting the depeg
(we monitor it); on mainnet we plan Chainlink feeds for USDT/USDC so a depeg blocks operations automatically.
Disclosed in `DISCLOSURES.md` D-5 and D-2. Tests: `test/unit/V52.t.sol` `test_AA1_*`.

### AA-2 (Medium) — freeze gap — **Fixed**
`_mintFreeze` now checks whether the interval `[now, now + mintFreezeSec]` reaches 00:00 UTC on the 28th (instead of
sampling only the point `now + mintFreezeSec`). The freeze is contiguous for any allowed value; the maximum stays
2 days. Tests: hour-by-hour sweep with a 2-day freeze and a fuzz test over all values (`test_AA2_*`, `testFuzz_AA2_*`).

### AA-3 (Medium) — working-price sandwich — **Acknowledged**
Same issue as our earlier internal finding M2. The owner is a Safe 2-of-3; working-price changes are monitored.
Operational rule: change the working price in steps of at most 0.5% (below the 0.6% round-trip fee, so a single-step
sandwich is unprofitable) and do not disclose transaction parameters in advance. Profit per sandwich is also bounded by
the mint limiter (150% coverage floor, hourly and per-block caps). A code-level step limit was considered and left as an
owner decision because it changes the operating workflow. Disclosed in `DISCLOSURES.md` D-1.

### AA-4 (Medium) — hourly cap bypass — **Fixed**
The decaying previous-window counter was replaced by seven 10-minute buckets. Every mint younger than one hour is
counted in full, so total minting in any trailing hour cannot exceed the cap (memory is 60–70 minutes; no decay).
Tests: the report's scenario and a fuzz test over random mint sequences (`test_AA4_*`, `testFuzz_AA4_*`).

### AA-5 (Medium) — sequencer feed — **Fixed**
The constructor and `setSequencerUptimeFeed` now require a semantically valid response (`answer` 0 or 1, `startedAt`
non-zero and not in the future). New `disableBrokenSequencerFeed()`: the owner or guardian can disable the check
instantly, **only if the installed feed returns an invalid response** — a healthy feed reporting downtime or a grace
period cannot be bypassed. Tests: `test_AA5_*`.

### AA-6 (Low) — previews vs execution — **Fixed**
`previewMint` now reverts with the same errors as `mint`: pause, frozen stable, sequencer, pre-index freeze,
unavailable feed / depeg, hourly and per-block caps, coverage floor and genesis top-up. `quoteRedeem.executableNow`
uses the same price validity rule as `redeem` and checks the sequencer. `isDepegged` and the `StableDepegStatusChanged`
signal treat an unavailable feed as unusable instead of falling back to the manual price. `stablePrice` still shows the
fallback value for display. Remaining note: for fee-on-transfer tokens the preview is computed on the nominal amount
(documented). Tests: `test_AA6_*`.

### AA-7 (Low) — fee push — **Fixed**
Protocol fees are now accrued in the vault (`accruedFees[stable]`) and sent to the fee recipient by a separate,
permissionless `claimFees(stable)`. A blacklisted or reverting fee recipient can no longer block mint or redemption.
Invariant: vault balance = reserves + accrued fees. Tests: `test_AA7_*`, invariant suite.
